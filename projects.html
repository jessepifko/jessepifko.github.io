Faith, Family, Freedom

I work to provide for my family and to make a difference — both are non-negotiable, and neither comes at the expense of the other. As someone called to be salt and light, I bring integrity, service, and honesty into every role, not as separate from my faith but as an expression of it. I do my best work within a clear, shared mission that everyone is accountable to. Structure isn't bureaucracy to me — it's the ground that makes real leadership and collaboration possible. Give me that mission, along with freedom and trust to operate, and I will move things forward. My process is quiet before it's loud. I study situations carefully and test ideas independently before I bring them to others — not because I don't value collaboration, but because I don't trust slow processes to get to the right answer fast enough on their own. When I do bring an idea into the room, it's already been vetted. I'm energized by building things and by challenging the status quo — asking the questions others hesitate to ask, pushing people to reconsider their assumptions, and seeing better ideas win out. That full arc — question, disrupt, land — is where I do my most meaningful work. I want to be known as someone who is smart, reliable, and motivated — someone whose work moves things forward and leaves people better for having worked alongside them.
